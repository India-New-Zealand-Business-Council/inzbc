Communication job
By the end, INZBC and course assessors should understand that the team produced a strong governed implementation whose next phase is validation and readiness work, not immediate handover.

Theory applied to evidence
- Sociotechnical systems: technical controls and human roles must be designed together (slides 4 and 6).
- Conway's Law: workstream communication boundaries shaped the platform boundaries (slides 7 and 17).
- Agile / continuous delivery: small increments, CI and review created rapid feedback without implying production release (slides 10-12 and 14).
- Double-loop learning: the team changed governing assumptions and controls, not only individual defects (slides 13 and 16).
- Psychological safety: risks, mistakes and unknown facts were surfaced through review and client escalation (slides 15 and 17).

Readiness correction
- Replace all claims of immediate handover with continued UAT, client decisions, board reconciliation, operating-model definition and readiness evidence.
- Keep handover only as a later milestone that follows those gates.
