# Module — Sponsors & trade services

Owner: Bhanu · Status: planned · Internal (not public).

## Purpose
The commercial engine: sponsor pipeline + benefit delivery, and the trade-service requests and
introductions INZBC facilitates. Internal records; only approved profiles appear publicly.

## Sponsor records
Agreement term, financial + in-kind value, tier, promised benefits, owner, delivery dates,
evidence of delivery, renewal date, relationship notes, consent + communication rules.
Pipeline: prospect → proposal → contract → delivery → renewal.

## Trade-service & introduction records
Request purpose, requesting organisation, sector + market, consent to share, assigned owner,
actions, introductions made, outcome, confidentiality classification, closure reason.

## Why it matters
This is where the platform earns its keep — measurable outcomes: introductions made, deals
supported, barriers resolved, delegations formed, benefits delivered on time.

## Dependencies
Bhanu's DB + API; CRM/system-of-record decision; INZBC sponsorship tiers + benefits.

## Open decisions
Where records live (Microsoft 365 Lists/Power Apps vs repo service); larger CRM only after fields,
users and consent are defined.

## Definition of done
Sponsor pipeline + benefit delivery tracked with evidence; trade-service requests owned and closed
with outcome; confidentiality enforced; renewal reminders; board-ready reporting.
