# About

## Mission
INZBC advances trade, investment and enduring commercial ties between New Zealand and India. We
advise government on the bilateral relationship, share market knowledge with members, and connect
New Zealand and Indian businesses.

## History
Founded in 1988, INZBC is New Zealand's longstanding India-trade body. [[key milestones since 1988
— confirm dates and events with INZBC]]. Recognised by the governments of both countries.

## What we do
- Advocate on trade policy and market access affecting members.
- Provide sourced market intelligence (see the Trade Intelligence Digest and FTA Centre).
- Facilitate business development, delegations and introductions.
- Convene events, including the annual INZBC Summit.
