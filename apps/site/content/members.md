## Hero
**Headline:** Turn the NZ–India FTA into an advantage for your business.
**Sub:** INZBC membership gives you a direct line into the trade relationship — advocacy, market
intelligence, and the networks and events that turn FTA access into real deals.

## Why join
- **Advocacy** — a direct voice with government on trade policy, market access and FTA
  implementation, so member concerns reach the people setting the rules.
- **FTA and market intelligence** — the Trade Intelligence Digest and FTA sector briefings, sourced
  and human-reviewed before publication, so you can act on what's actually changed.
- **Networks** — business development support, delegations and introductions across the NZ–India
  corridor.
- **Events and access** — sector briefings, the annual INZBC Summit, and opportunities to engage
  with visiting government delegations and trade missions.
